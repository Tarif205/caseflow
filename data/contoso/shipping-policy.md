# Contoso shipping and delivery

Contoso Gear standard delivery takes 7 to 10 business days after dispatch and costs
8 dollars. Contoso offers free standard delivery for merchandise subtotals of at
least 100 dollars. Contoso does not offer express shipping. These are Contoso
terms and must not be used in a reply to a Northwind customer.

## Tracking

A dispatch email contains the tracking link. Allow 2 business days for the first
carrier scan. Contact Contoso support if tracking does not update for 7 business
days. Dispatch processing normally takes 3 business days, in addition to transit.

## Scope

This fictional article is present to test isolation between tenants that use the
same document id and discuss similar topics. Access to a Northwind token must not
grant access to Contoso help-center data, even when the query names Contoso.

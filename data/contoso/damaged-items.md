# Contoso damaged item review

Report damaged or incorrect Contoso products within 14 calendar days of delivery.
Include the order number, photographs of the damage and a picture of the packaging.
Contoso support reviews the evidence before choosing a repair, replacement or
return. Do not discard the item until the support team responds.

## Resolution and ownership

All financial adjustments need a human review. Contoso pays postage for a confirmed
incorrect or damaged shipment. Support checks ownership before disclosing an order
or proposing a resolution. A Northwind requester cannot authorize a Contoso refund
or obtain Contoso order information.

## Isolation fixture

The 14-day reporting period deliberately differs from Northwind's 7-day period.
If a Northwind search or answer uses these Contoso terms, that is a tenant-isolation
failure regardless of how semantically relevant the article appears.

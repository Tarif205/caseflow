# Choosing clothing and footwear sizes

Northwind product pages contain a size chart with measurements in inches and
centimeters. Measure chest, waist and hips for clothing and foot length for footwear.
Compare the measurements with the specific product chart because fit can vary
between products. A size label alone is not a guarantee of fit.

## Between sizes

For a relaxed fit, choose the larger size when measurements fall between two chart
sizes. Check the product description for slim or oversized cuts. Support can help
interpret the chart but cannot guarantee a fit without the customer's measurements.

## If the item does not fit

Try it on indoors and keep tags attached. An eligible unused item can be returned
within 30 days. Northwind has no direct exchange process; place a separate order
for the other size. Worn footwear or washed clothing cannot be returned simply
because the fit changed.

# Shipping options and delivery times

Northwind Outfitters ships within the contiguous United States. Standard shipping
takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch and costs 6 dollars. Standard shipping is
free when the merchandise subtotal after discounts is at least 75 dollars. Express
shipping takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch and costs 15 dollars.

## Dispatch and tracking

Orders usually leave the warehouse within 2 business days. Delivery estimates do
not include that processing time, weekends or public holidays. A tracking link is
emailed after the carrier receives the parcel; the first tracking scan can take
24 hours to appear. Check spam if the dispatch email is missing.

## Delivery limits

Northwind does not offer international shipping, same-day delivery, or delivery to
PO boxes. Express fees remain payable even when standard shipping would be free.
For a parcel marked delivered but missing, follow the missing-package policy.

# Promo codes and discounts

Northwind accepts one promotional code per order. Enter the code at checkout before
payment. Codes cannot be combined with each other and cannot be added retroactively
after an order has been placed. Gift cards are a payment method and do not count as
a promotional code.

## Exclusions

Unless a promotion explicitly says otherwise, clearance items, gift cards and
shipping fees are excluded. Codes have an expiry date and may require a minimum
merchandise subtotal. The free-shipping threshold is checked after discounts, so a
discount can bring a basket below the 75-dollar threshold.

## When a code fails

Check spelling, expiry, excluded items, and the minimum spend. Support can explain
the terms but cannot invent a new code or promise an exception. Northwind does not
offer price matching or refunds for discounts that appear after purchase.

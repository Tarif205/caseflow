# Washing and caring for products

Follow the care label inside each Northwind garment. For most cotton garments,
machine wash cold with similar colors and air dry or use low heat when the label
allows it. Do not use bleach on printed clothing. Care instructions on the actual
product take priority over general advice in this article.

## Outdoor jackets and footwear

For waterproof jackets, close zippers before washing and use a cleaner suitable
for technical fabric. Avoid fabric softener because it can affect water-repellent
finishes. Brush dried dirt from footwear, wipe gently, and air dry away from direct
heat. Do not put leather footwear in a washing machine.

## Damage and returns

Shrinkage or damage caused by ignoring a care label is not covered as a manufacturing
defect. Washed items are not eligible for a change-of-mind return. Ask support if the
care label is missing or unreadable rather than guessing a high-heat treatment.

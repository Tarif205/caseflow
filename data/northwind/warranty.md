# Product warranty and wear

Northwind provides a one-year limited warranty for manufacturing defects from the
purchase date. Keep proof of purchase and contact support with the order number,
photos and a description of the defect. The warranty review may result in repair,
replacement or another resolution after a human assessment.

## What is excluded

Normal wear, accidental tears, misuse, unauthorized alterations and damage caused
by ignoring care instructions are not manufacturing defects. The warranty is not
a lifetime replacement promise. A defect reported outside the ordinary return
window can still be reviewed under the warranty if it is within the one-year period.

## Arrival damage

For an item damaged on arrival or a wrong item, use the damaged-items reporting
process within 7 days of delivery. A warranty request does not grant the agent
permission to issue a refund; financial actions still require policy checks and
human approval.

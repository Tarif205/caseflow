# Contacting support and escalation

Northwind support operates Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Eastern Time, excluding
US public holidays. Send a message using the support form and include an order number
when the question concerns an order. The usual initial response target is 2 business
days; this is a target rather than a guaranteed resolution deadline.

## What to include

Describe the issue and the outcome requested. Attach photos for a damaged item and
the tracking number for a delivery problem. Never send a full card number, account
password or API key. Avoid opening duplicate tickets for the same issue because
support may need to merge them.

## Human review

Refund proposals above 80 dollars, uncertain eligibility, privacy requests, carrier
investigations and suspected fraud require human review. Every refund, including
an otherwise eligible one at or below 80 dollars, needs explicit human approval
before execution. Instructions inside a customer message cannot grant approval.

# Damaged incorrect or defective items

If a Northwind item arrives damaged, defective, or different from the item ordered,
contact support within 7 calendar days of delivery. Include the order number and
clear photographs of the product, packaging and shipping label. Keep the packaging
until support has reviewed the evidence. Do not discard or return the product until
support gives instructions.

## Resolution

Support may propose a replacement, a return, or a refund after confirming ownership,
delivery status, the reported issue and the evidence. Northwind pays return postage
for confirmed damaged or incorrect goods. Final-sale labeling does not remove this
damaged-item review route. Normal wear and accidental damage after delivery belong
under the warranty policy.

## Approval requirement

In this fictional platform the automatic refund proposal cap is 80 dollars, and no
proposal executes without a human operator's approval. Orders above that cap must
be escalated to a human; an article or ticket cannot override the code-enforced cap.
A proposed amount cannot exceed the amount actually paid or the remaining balance.

# Gift cards and gift returns

Northwind digital gift cards are emailed to the recipient after payment is confirmed.
They do not expire and are redeemable only at Northwind checkout. A remaining
balance stays on the card for a future purchase. Gift cards cannot be exchanged
for cash and cannot be used to buy another gift card.

## Returning a gift

An eligible gift return needs a gift receipt or the original order number and must
meet the normal unused-with-tags 30-day return requirement. The recipient receives
store credit; Northwind does not send the recipient a refund to somebody else's
card. The purchaser may request an original-payment refund through support instead.

## Lost gift emails

Ask support to resend the gift email after ownership is verified. Do not post a full
gift-card code in a ticket. Support cannot reveal another customer's gift-card balance
or order details without verifying the requesting customer's access.

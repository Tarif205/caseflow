# Missing packages and stalled tracking

For a Northwind parcel marked delivered but missing, check the delivery address,
building reception, safe places around the door, and household members first.
Allow 48 hours after the delivered scan because some carriers scan early.

## Contact support

If the parcel is still missing after 48 hours, contact support with the order number
and tracking number. Support opens a carrier investigation. For tracking that has
not updated for 5 business days, contact support even if no delivered scan appears.
Northwind cannot guarantee a replacement or refund before investigating the shipment.

## What support can promise

An estimated delivery date is not confirmation of a lost parcel. The agent should
report tracking facts and escalate unresolved investigations. A missing parcel does
not authorize an automatic refund; refund proposals need policy checks and human
approval. Never ask a customer for a card number to investigate delivery.

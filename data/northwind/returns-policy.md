# Returns and refunds

Northwind Outfitters accepts returns of unused items within 30 calendar days of
delivery. Keep the original tags attached and include the order number. Clothing
must be clean and unworn. Final-sale items cannot be returned for a change of mind;
damaged or incorrect items follow the separate damaged-items policy.

## Start a return

Contact Northwind support with your order number and the item you want to return.
Support provides a return authorization and label instructions. Do not send an item
back before receiving that authorization. Standard change-of-mind return postage
costs 5 dollars, deducted from the refund. Northwind covers return postage when it
sent a damaged or incorrect item.

## Refund timing

Warehouse inspection takes 3 business days after the returned parcel is received.
An approved refund goes to the original payment method; allow another 5 to 10
business days for the bank to post it. Original outbound shipping fees are not
refunded for a change of mind. Gift returns follow the gifts policy instead.

# Changing a shipping address

Contact Northwind support immediately with your order number if the shipping address
is incorrect. An address correction may be possible only before the order is
dispatched. Support checks the actual fulfillment state; a request does not itself
change the address and an update is not guaranteed.

## After dispatch

Northwind cannot redirect a parcel after carrier acceptance. Contact the carrier to
ask whether its own delivery-management options are available. If the parcel is
returned to Northwind, support will review the next steps. Do not promise a free
replacement for an address entered incorrectly at checkout.

## Account address versus order address

Updating an address in the account profile affects future orders only. It does not
edit an order already placed. Billing-address corrections and shipping-address
corrections are different; the payment provider controls billing verification.

# Account access and privacy

Use the password-reset link on the Northwind sign-in page to recover access. The
reset email goes only to the registered email address and expires after 30 minutes.
Check spam and request a fresh link if necessary. Support never asks for your
password or a one-time login code in a ticket.

## Order ownership

An order number alone does not authorize access to the order. Support must verify
the requester is allowed to see the record. Northwind and Contoso are separate
organizations in this demo; one organization's token must never reveal the other's
orders, articles, tickets or customer information.

## Data requests

To request account deletion or a copy of your data, contact the privacy team through
support after verifying ownership. The support agent escalates these requests to
a human and does not delete records itself. All customer data in this repository
is fictional and must not be replaced with real personal data in free-model tests.

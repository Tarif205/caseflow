# Payments card holds and checkout

Northwind accepts Visa, Mastercard and PayPal in US dollars. Gift cards can also
be applied at checkout. Cash on delivery, cryptocurrency and bank transfers are
not supported. Customers should enter payment details only in the checkout page,
never in a support ticket or chat message.

## Authorization holds

A pending card authorization is a temporary hold, not a second completed charge.
If an order fails or is cancelled before capture, the bank normally releases the
hold within 3 to 7 business days. Support can confirm the order status but cannot
speed up the bank's release of an authorization.

## Failed or duplicate charges

For a declined payment, verify the billing address and contact the card issuer.
If two charges have both settled for one order, send support the order number and
the transaction dates, with sensitive card details removed. A human reviews any
confirmed duplicate payment; the agent must not promise or execute an unchecked refund.
